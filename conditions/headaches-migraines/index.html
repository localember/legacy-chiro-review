Headache and migraine relief in Cary

Many headaches start in the neck. Tight upper neck joints and muscles can refer pain into the back of the head, the temples or behind the eyes.

Book a free consultCall (919) 363-2277

Tension and neck-related headaches

These often build through the day and feel like a band or pressure. They are frequently linked to posture and stiffness in the top of the neck.

What we look for

We check how the upper neck moves and how the head sits over the shoulders, then adjust only the segments that need it.

Keep a simple log

Write down when headaches happen, what you were doing, sleep and water intake. It helps us spot patterns. Sudden, severe or unusual headaches need medical attention right away.
