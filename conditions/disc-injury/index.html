Disc injury care in Cary

Discs are the cushions between your vertebrae. When one bulges or herniates, it can irritate nearby nerves and make simple movements painful.

Book a free consultCall (919) 363-2277

Bulging versus herniated

A bulge is when the disc spreads outward. A herniation means some of the inner material has pushed through the outer layer. Both can cause back or neck pain and symptoms into an arm or leg.

Why Gonstead fits disc cases

Dr. Clarence Gonstead built his method around the disc. The goal of each adjustment is to help the vertebra sit level on the disc below it, using careful positioning and very little twisting.

Imaging first

Full-spine X-rays help us see disc height and alignment before we decide on care. If you need an MRI or a referral, we will say so.
