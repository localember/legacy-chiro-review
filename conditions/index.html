Where it hurts, and why

Conditions we help in Cary

Pain is a signal, and the source is not always where you feel it. Here is how we think about the complaints people bring to our Cary office most often.

Book a free consultCall (919) 363-2277

Back pain reliefMost back pain is mechanical, which means a joint, disc or muscle is not moving the way it should.Neck pain reliefPhones, laptops and long commutes all pull the head forward.Sciatica reliefSciatica is a description, not a diagnosis.Headache and migraine reliefMany headaches start in the neck.Disc injury careDiscs are the cushions between your vertebrae.Shoulder pain reliefA sore shoulder is not always a shoulder problem.Knee pain reliefThe knee sits between the hip and the foot, so it often takes the blame for problems above and below it.Arm and leg pain reliefPain, tingling or weakness in an arm or leg often traces back to a nerve that leaves the spine in the neck or low back.Carpal tunnel reliefNumb fingers and a tingling hand at night are classic signs.Scoliosis careScoliosis is a sideways curve of the spine.Vertigo and dizziness careDizziness has many causes, from the inner ear to blood pressure.

How every plan starts

  1. TalkA free 15-minute conversation about your history and goals.
  2. ExaminePosture scan, temperature reading, hands-on checks and full-spine X-rays when needed.
  3. ExplainWe show you what we found and lay out a written plan.
  4. AdjustSpecific, low-force adjustments where the findings agree.
