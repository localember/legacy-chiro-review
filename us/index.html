About us

Meet the Legacy Chiropractic team in Cary

Dr. Ballam opened Legacy Chiropractic on Davis Drive in 2012. The name comes from a simple idea: when you feel well, you are free to live to leave your mark.

Book a free consultCall (919) 363-2277

Dr. John Ballam, D.C.

Founder, chiropractor

John started Legacy Chiropractic in Cary in 2012 because he believes people are built for health and deserve more than symptom management. He looks for the cause and takes the time to explain it.

  • Bachelor of Arts in Psychology, Gordon College
  • Pre-medical studies, Des Moines Area Community College
  • Doctor of Chiropractic, Northwestern Health Sciences University
  • 100+ hours of Gonstead continuing education
  • Pediatric short lever technique, International Chiropractic Pediatric Association
  • Member, Gonstead Clinical Studies Society and North Carolina Chiropractic Association

Off the clock you will find him mountain biking, fishing, gardening or cooking with his wife Elena, and spending time with their daughters Brielle and Elise.

Dr. Adam Schnebelt, D.C.

Chiropractor

Adam is passionate about the body's built-in ability to heal and about handing patients back control of their health.

  • Bachelor of Science in Exercise Science, UNC Charlotte
  • Doctor of Chiropractic, Sherman College of Chiropractic
  • 250+ hours of Gonstead continuing education
  • Member, Gonstead Clinical Studies Society and North Carolina Chiropractic Association

He volunteers with local animal shelters and likes hiking, the Cary greenways, sports and time with family, friends and his partner Daisy.

Angie Kautz

Office manager

Angie was a patient before she joined the team, so she knows the experience from both sides of the desk. Outside the office she sings, serves at church and in the community, plays Nintendo and never says no to a British crime show.
