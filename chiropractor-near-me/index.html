Chiropractic is one of the most rewarding fields in health care, yet many people are not sure what it involves. Here is a quick look at what our doctors do and why so many local families rely on us.

What chiropractic care does

Chiropractors correct misalignments of the spine to treat a broad range of physical problems, including muscle strain, neck pain, long-term back pain and conditions like sciatica. Adjustments work by moving the spine back toward its proper shape. Because it involves no drugs or surgery, most people find it a comfortable way to get relief.

More than a back treatment

Many people assume chiropractic only helps backs. In fact it can help with problems you might never connect to your spine, including headaches and migraines, chronic pain, carpal tunnel syndrome, and pain from accidents or muscle strain.

Where the word comes from

"Chiropractic" comes from two Greek words, cheir (hand) and praktos (done), meaning "done by hand." Daniel David Palmer, the founder of chiropractic, chose them. In 1895 he adjusted a janitor named Harvey Lillard, who was mostly deaf at the time, and Lillard later reported that his hearing improved after the adjustment. Palmer opened the first chiropractic school two years later. Over the century since, chiropractors have used adjustments for headaches and migraines, strained muscles, sore backs, carpal tunnel and much more, and his work still touches millions of lives.
