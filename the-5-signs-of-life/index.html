The 5 signs of life, explained by a Cary chiropractor

With Halloween around the corner, it is a fitting week to ask a spooky question: how can you tell something is truly alive? Biology answers with five signs of life.

1. Assimilation

You have probably heard "you are what you eat." It is closer to the truth to say you are what you absorb. Assimilation is your body's ability to take in nutrients and put them to work for growth, upkeep and building new tissue. When the body cannot do this, tissues go hungry, which leads to malnourishment and starvation at the cellular level.

2. Excretion

Every healthy cell makes waste, and that waste has to go somewhere. Your body has dedicated routes for detoxifying and clearing it out. Without them, toxins would build up and cells would struggle and eventually die.

3. Adaptability

Living things respond to whatever forces come their way. Keeping your core temperature steady on a cold morning is adaptability. So is a muscle getting stronger as you ask more of it. When the body fails to adapt to stress, tissue breaks down, illness follows, and ultimately the result is death.

4. Growth

Growth means developing to maturity by following an intelligent plan. Each of your cells grows within set limits, in both size and direction. When cells grow too little or too much, it points to poor coordination inside the body.

5. Reproduction

Staying alive and making more of itself: those are the two core tasks of any organism. Your body relies on cell reproduction to replace damaged and worn-out tissue. That process is not random; it runs under intelligent control. Without that control, disease would be the only possible outcome. Cancer is an example: cancer cells ignore the needs of the body and work only for their own survival.

Where chiropractic fits

These five signs depend on coordination within the body, and that coordination is what creates health. A subluxation in the spine reduces that coordination, so the body expresses less life.
