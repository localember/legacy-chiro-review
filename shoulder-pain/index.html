Shoulder pain relief in Cary

A sore shoulder is not always a shoulder problem. The neck and upper back control a lot of what the shoulder does.

Book a free consultCall (919) 363-2277

Common sources

Rounded posture, stiff upper back joints and irritated nerves from the neck can all show up as shoulder pain, along with the shoulder joint itself.

How we test it

We check the shoulder's range of motion along with the neck and upper back, so we can tell which area is really driving the pain.

At home

Avoid sleeping on the sore side and keep the arm moving gently within a comfortable range unless your doctor tells you otherwise.
