## Affordable chiropractic in Cary is real
Chiropractic is often much more affordable than people expect, even without insurance. Fair pricing matters to us at Legacy Chiropractic. What we charge per adjustment is in line with many people's insurance copays, and sometimes lower, so most people can afford the care they need even without coverage. We also offer family plans so the whole household can be cared for, along with affordable payment plans.

## Why some offices cost more
Chiropractic gets expensive at some offices when other therapies, such as electric stimulation, laser or spinal decompression, are bundled with adjustments. Our goal is to deliver the best chiropractic adjustments in Cary and get to the root of your symptoms. If we think you need another kind of therapy, we gladly refer you to one of the physical therapists we work with. Most of the time, though, we find chiropractic care alone relieves the symptoms our patients come in with, which keeps care affordable.

## Think of it as an investment
Chiropractic can save you an enormous amount compared with what untreated symptoms may cost over time: over-the-counter medicine, prescriptions, surgery, ER visits and MRIs, not to mention missed work and missed life. Investing now saves you later.
