H1: Hearty Health Salad, a favorite recipe from our Cary chiropractic family
Want a healthy meal with lots of flavor? Try the Hearty Health Salad. It is loaded with vegetables and protein, so it fills you up and keeps you going. We have made it more times than we can count and it is still a favorite. Chickpeas, hardboiled eggs, avocado and bacon make it filling enough to be lunch or dinner on its own.

## Salad
- Mixed greens
- Romaine hearts
- 2 carrots
- 4 hardboiled eggs
- 1 avocado
- 1 cup grape tomatoes
- 1 cup chickpeas
- 1 bell pepper
- Bacon to taste

## Lemon vinaigrette
- 1 Tbsp dijon mustard
- 1/4 cup lemon juice
- 1 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup olive oil
- 1 clove garlic, finely chopped

## Putting it together
Combine equal parts mixed greens and chopped romaine hearts. Pile on the toppings, add the dressing and dig in.

Honestly, we rarely measure; we just toss everything in. Use the amounts above as a starting point, and swap vegetables to suit your taste. Watermelon radishes look especially pretty.
