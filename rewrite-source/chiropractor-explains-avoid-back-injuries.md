H1: How to avoid back injuries, from a Cary chiropractor
Avoiding back injuries is mostly up to you. A surprise fall or a car accident can hurt you no matter what, but a large share of back injuries come from things we do ourselves, which means you can prevent them or keep them from getting worse. Here are some useful tips.

## Lift the right way
How you pick up boxes, laundry baskets, toys, a pair of shoes or almost anything else affects your back. With good form, your spine stays aligned and takes no extra stress. Bend your knees and keep your back as straight as you can whenever you lift something off the floor.

If you have a lot to lift, set items on a table or chair first so you do not have to bend as far. Your back was not built to work like a crane, and using it that way adds stress it does not need. Keep your weight even, let your legs do the work, and turn with your feet, not your waist, when you are carrying a load.

## Move with your back in mind
Doing things with your back's health in mind is a great way to avoid injury. Stretch before any physical activity, pace yourself when there is a lot of repetitive lifting, and take breaks to rest and stretch along the way.

Getting in good shape protects your back too. Losing weight around your middle and strengthening your core make lifting and everyday movement easier on your back. Ask nearly any chiropractor in Cary and you will hear that a firm mattress helps protect your back too.

## Do not let injuries linger
If you hurt your back despite your best efforts, see a Cary chiropractor as soon as you can. Left untreated, a misaligned spine, a bulging or herniated disc and similar injuries tend to worsen. Prompt care restores blood flow to the area, relieves pinched nerves and gets you back to normal as quickly as possible.
