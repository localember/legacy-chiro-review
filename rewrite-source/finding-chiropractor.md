If you have searched online or in the local directory for chiropractic care in Cary, you have probably found plenty of options. That is where it gets harder. What makes one Cary chiropractor different from another, and how do you find the best one for you?

It can be tricky, but a few things really do set chiropractors apart. Knowing them will help you find the right fit and, as a result, get better results from care. Here are questions to ask and things to look at as you decide.

## Tips for finding the best chiropractor in Cary
1. <b>Start with people you trust.</b> Before turning to the internet or a directory, ask friends and relatives you trust about their experiences and who they would go to if they needed help. Referrals say a lot about how an office operates.
2. <b>Book a consultation.</b> Once you have a short list, schedule a first visit and bring your questions. Research shows that results at any health care office depend not only on the care itself but on how comfortable the patient feels with the doctor.
3. <b>Ask about technique.</b> Many people do not know chiropractors use a range of techniques. Most learn several and refine one or more to suit each patient's condition, so ask how the doctor adjusts. Adjustments may be done by hand or with various instruments. Some are so light you feel only a slight sensation; others produce a popping sound called a cavitation. Be sure you are comfortable with both the doctor and their adjusting style.
4. <b>Check their education.</b> Every chiropractor goes through demanding training, but there are many chiropractic schools. Make sure the chiropractor you are considering graduated from a school accredited by the Council on Chiropractic Education. It also helps to find out whether they hold additional certifications.
