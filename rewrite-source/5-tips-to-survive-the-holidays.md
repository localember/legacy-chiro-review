TITLE: 5 Tips to Survive the Holidays in Cary
H1: Five ways to get through the holidays in Cary feeling well
The holidays can wear anyone out. Rich food, family tension, money worries and travel add up, and many of us limp into January exhausted. These five habits can help.
- <b>Eat for fuel.</b> What your body takes in and absorbs becomes part of you. The season is full of treats, so lean on moderation: enjoy a slice of pie, not the whole thing. Dark leafy greens and fruit are packed with antioxidants that help offset stress.
- <b>Aim for 7 hours of sleep.</b> Your body does most of its growing and repairing while you sleep. It also makes cortisol, a hormone that helps you cope with stress, and sleep is needed to restock it.
- <b>Move.</b> Exercise is a natural stress reliever. It triggers endorphins and enkephalins, the feel-good chemicals behind the phrase "runner's high." A brisk 30-minute walk each day is within reach for most people.
- <b>Look for the joy.</b> You may not dodge every difficult relative or every obligatory party, but you can choose optimism. We do not always control our circumstances, but we do control our response. You do not have to please everyone.
- <b>Remember you are loved.</b> You are wonderful, and there is only one you.
