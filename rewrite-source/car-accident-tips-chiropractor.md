H1: Car accident tips from a Cary chiropractor
Many of us will be in a car accident at some point. Most are minor, but even small crashes can leave lasting effects. Back, neck and shoulder pain can hang on and turn into chronic problems that last for years. These tips can help limit the damage.

## Prevention comes first
The surest way to avoid crash injuries is to avoid crashes. You cannot prevent every accident, but you can lower your odds. The most practical step is to put your phone away, out of sight, and keep both hands on the wheel.

Avoid driving when you are exhausted, upset or angry. Strong emotions and drowsiness behind the wheel invite trouble. If you drive in changing weather, adjust to the conditions and stay a little under the speed limit when you can. Keep your brakes and tires in good shape.

## A fit body recovers faster
Staying in good physical condition is another tip worth following. When your body is strong and flexible, recovering from most accidents is much easier. No one plans to crash, but a healthy body helps here too, along with all its other benefits. Any regular exercise that builds strength and flexibility is a good choice.

## Get checked right away
Whatever shape you are in, get your injuries examined as soon as you can after an accident. If you wait, scar tissue can become a permanent part of your muscles and tendons, limiting your motion and causing pain.
