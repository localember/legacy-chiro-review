## What chiropractors look for
Chiropractors are trained to find spinal misalignments that interfere with the health and function of the nervous system. These are called subluxations, and they happen when the body cannot overcome physical, chemical or emotional and mental stress. This article focuses on the physical stresses that can cause subluxations in newborns.

## Stress before birth
A baby's spine can first be stressed in the womb. Before birth, a baby grows seven times faster than it will after delivery, and that rapid growth makes it especially vulnerable to constraining and molding forces. Those forces can come from the mother's tissues as they shear, stretch, compress, twist and bend. A misaligned pelvis during pregnancy can add to the normal stress. Any of these can affect how the baby's spine develops. The baby's position can also change the spine; four of the most common positions are breech, face, brow and transverse lie.

## Stress during birth
Delivery can bring new trauma. Every mom hopes for a smooth, safe birth, but complications happen, and even moms who do everything right can have unexpected stress placed on the baby's spine. Birth trauma can come from manual delivery, forceps or a vacuum extractor. The neck is most often injured when it is pulled while extended back, rotated, flexed or bent to the side. Dr. Gutmann, a medical and manual medicine physician, studied 1,000 infants with birth trauma and found that pulling forces often caused upper neck subluxations. He suggested that the resulting nerve dysfunction might weaken children's immune systems and contribute to common childhood illnesses such as ear infections.

## Planning ahead
The best time to start planning your newborn's chiropractic care is with mom, before she conceives. When her spine, pelvis and nervous system are working their best, the baby gets the best start.

## The newborn screening
After birth, the screening evaluates the structure and function of the baby's spine. The chiropractor reviews the birth history, checks reflexes, measures range of motion and looks for signs of nervous system stress. The spine gets special attention: the chiropractor observes posture, feels along the spine, and uses a nervoscope to detect the temperature changes linked to subluxations. The exam is very gentle, and any subluxations found are corrected by hand. Chiropractic is very safe for patients of every age and size, and with babies and children the chiropractor takes great care to use only the lightest touch.

## Through childhood
Chiropractic helps throughout childhood. After birth, a baby's spine will more than double in size, and healthy spinal development is crucial during the first six years of rapid growth. Children should be monitored and checked for subluxations so their spines and nervous systems have the best chance to develop well.
