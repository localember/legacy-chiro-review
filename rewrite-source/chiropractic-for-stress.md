Your body is wired to answer stress in one of two ways: fight or flight.

That reaction exists to get you through danger, like being chased by a tiger, and it served early humans well. Our stressors today are different: money, a demanding boss, a strained relationship, technology, traffic, losing someone we love, moving and more. Actually fighting or running is not a fitting response to most of these, and in some cases it would be illegal. So the stress builds up inside the body and wears on our health.

## What chronic stress does
Over time, chronic stress can speed up aging, reduce insulin sensitivity, lower libido, slow wound healing, raise your heart rate and push up your blood pressure.

## Work with your biology
How do we respect the way we are built when everyday stress hits? The healthiest response is to move. Even a simple walk lets your body complete that fight-or-flight cycle.

## Where chiropractic helps
Chiropractic is an excellent way to help your body adapt to stress, for three reasons:
- An adjustment creates healthy movement in the spine, which checks the movement box.
- An adjustment lets your brain release endorphins and enkephalins, the body's natural feel-good chemicals. That is one reason patients often feel relaxed right after being adjusted.
- Above all, adjustments take pressure off the nervous system, freeing it to run and coordinate everything your body does more efficiently.
