## Your reading is 130/80. What does that tell you?
Most of us know the routine at a checkup: roll up a sleeve, hold out an arm, and wait for the cuff to squeeze. Each number measures something different happening inside your arteries. The top number, systolic, is the pressure your heart creates when it contracts and pushes blood through your vessels. The bottom number, diastolic, is the pressure between beats, which tells your doctor how much resistance your blood vessels have.

## Where chiropractic comes in
Chiropractors are trained in how the structure of your spine affects the function of your nervous system. Between each pair of vertebrae, a nerve exits on the left and another on the right, and each nerve has a specific job. When a vertebra shifts out of place and presses on a nerve, that is a subluxation. So when one of these misalignments irritates a nerve involved in controlling blood pressure, it makes sense that your readings could swing out of the normal range.

## The research
A pilot study published in the Journal of Human Hypertension caught the medical community's attention. It found that restoring alignment of the atlas, the top bone of the neck, was associated with marked and lasting drops in blood pressure, on par with the effect of a two-drug combination therapy.
