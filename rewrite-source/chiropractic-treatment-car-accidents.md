Nobody plans on a car accident, yet nearly everyone who is in one feels some physical effect afterward. From a small fender bender to a serious multi-car pileup, your body takes the force. At our Cary office, car accident care is built around each person to get the most benefit.

## Common crash injuries
Neck, shoulder and back injuries are among the problems chiropractors see most after accidents. Whiplash happens when the cervical spine is suddenly snapped back and then forward, pushing it out of alignment. It often follows a rear-end collision the driver never saw coming, and stiffness and pain tend to build over the following days and weeks. On the other hand, people who see the impact coming often brace for it, which can leave them with strained back, neck or shoulder muscles.

## How we treat accident injuries
Your plan will match your specific injuries, but common parts of care include:
- Spinal adjustments
- Muscle stimulation or relaxation therapy
- Targeted exercises for the injured area
- Lifestyle advice to fix ergonomic habits that cause pain

Every car accident plan starts with a thorough consultation to pin down the main problems and the best way to solve them. Bodies differ and crashes differ, so care has to be individual to get lasting results.

## Treat the cause
Specific methods vary, but the guiding idea stays the same: deal with the cause first. Pain after an accident is often just covered up with medication. That may bring quick relief, but whatever is causing the pain is likely still there. Our Cary chiropractors look for and correct the underlying cause.
