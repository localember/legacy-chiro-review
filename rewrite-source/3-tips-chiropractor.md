TITLE: 3 Health Tips from a Cary Chiropractor
H1: Three health tips from a Cary chiropractor
Chiropractors see the effects of everyday habits up close, every day: aching backs, stiff necks and shoulders, poor sleep and more. So we have opinions about what helps. Here are three simple tips to keep your body running well.

## 1. Sit less
Sitting looks harmless, but long hours in a chair take a real toll. It has long been tied to back pain and spine problems, and newer research also links too much sitting to heart disease. If your job keeps you seated, get up and move at least once an hour. Take calls on your feet, try an adjustable standing desk, do a few knee bends or jumping jacks, or take a short walk. What matters is standing up and moving so the pressure comes off and you stay healthy.

## 2. Get injuries looked at quickly
A small twist or tweak that you ignore today can mean years of discomfort and muscles that do not work as they should. Ice is a smart first step to keep swelling down, but seeing a chiropractor soon afterward helps you heal and keeps your muscles and joints working fully.

A minor injury might not hurt much now, but you will feel it later. Many people end up relying on pain medicine, or moving less freely as they age, because they let an old injury go.

## 3. Stretch every day
Treating injuries early is good. Avoiding them is better. Daily stretching keeps muscles, tendons and ligaments supple, which helps prevent many common injuries. Make it part of your morning or add it to your workout. Muscles naturally tighten as we age, and hours bent over a desk shorten them further, which sets you up to get hurt. We suggest stretching your hamstrings, quads, calves, chest, hips and back. A few minutes a day pays off for life.
