TITLE: Anti-Discrimination Notice | Legacy Chiropractic Cary NC
H1: Anti-discrimination notice
Our office follows all applicable federal civil rights laws. We do not discriminate against anyone, exclude anyone, or treat anyone differently based on race, color, national origin, age, disability or sex.

## Filing a grievance
You may file a grievance with us in person, by mail, by fax or by email. If you would like help filing one, our doctors and staff are glad to assist.

## Filing a complaint with HHS
You may also file a civil rights complaint with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Office for Civil Rights. You can file online through the <a href="https://ocrportal.hhs.gov/ocr/portal/lobby.jsf">Office for Civil Rights Complaint Portal</a>, or by mail or phone:

U.S. Department of Health and Human Services<br>200 Independence Avenue, SW, Room 509F, HHH Building<br>Washington, D.C. 20201<br>1-800-368-1019, 800-537-7697 (TDD)

Complaint forms are available from the <a href="https://www.hhs.gov/ocr/complaints/index.html">HHS Office for Civil Rights</a>.
