Being physically fit and being well are not the same thing. Plenty of elite athletes look healthy and excel at their sport but would fail a full wellness check that looks at every part of well-being. Here is how we think about wellness care.

## Your physical body
When the parts of your health are out of balance, your body usually pays for it. Chronic illness and chronic pain shape many people's lives, and they do not always come from inactivity or a poor diet. Those things matter a great deal for keeping your body tuned up, but they are not the whole story.

## Emotional wellness
We often see patients whose chronic pain started with emotional trauma. Emotional wellness means letting go of past hurts and expressing yourself openly. Bottled-up emotional baggage has to go somewhere, and often it shows up as pain in your back and neck.

## Social wellness
An active social life and a circle of family and friends are another key part of total wellness. Some people are more introverted and need less social time, but everyone needs someone. Sharing both the good days and the hard ones with others matters.

## Bringing it together
When all of these areas are running well and working together, it is nearly impossible not to be healthy. Your Cary chiropractor cares for the whole person, not just the physical symptoms tied to bigger health problems. Chiropractic focuses on the underlying cause of those symptoms. Often that is a misaligned vertebra causing pain somewhere in the body, but it can be other things too.

As part of a complete wellness plan, the Legacy Chiropractic team will get to know you as a person and help sort out any parts of your wellness that are working against you.
