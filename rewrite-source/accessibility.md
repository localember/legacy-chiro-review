TITLE: Accessibility Statement | Legacy Chiropractic Cary NC
H1: Accessibility statement for Legacy Chiropractic in Cary
## Our commitment
Legacy Chiropractic wants everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use our services and our website. We believe each person deserves to live with dignity, equality, comfort and independence, and we have put real effort into making this site easier to use for people of all abilities.

## Tools on our site
Our current website offers the UserWay accessibility widget, which runs on its own accessibility server and helps us move toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.0). To use it, select the accessibility icon in the corner of the page and give the menu a moment to finish loading.

## Ongoing work
We keep working to improve the accessibility of our site and services, because we see seamless, unhindered access for people with disabilities as a shared responsibility. Even so, some pages or content on our site may not yet meet the strictest accessibility standards, sometimes because we have not yet found the right technical solution.

## We are here to help
If any part of our website is hard to use, or you need help with anything on it, please reach out during office hours and we will gladly assist.
