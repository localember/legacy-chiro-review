## Why do kids see a chiropractor?
First-time visitors sometimes do a double take when they spot the train table and the bins of toys in our waiting room. A few have even asked whether that many children really have low back pain. Most of the kids we see do not. They come in because a lot of the problems adults struggle with got their start in childhood.

Think about everything a growing body goes through: tumbles while learning to walk, youth sports, wrestling with brothers and sisters, overloaded backpacks, and long stretches at a desk or in front of a screen. It is common for children to pick up subluxations early. Left alone, those subluxations can lead over time to problems such as spinal degeneration, disc herniation, and many of the complaints that bring adults to a chiropractor.

## Prevention costs less
We look at pediatric chiropractic the way most families look at the dentist. Preventing a problem is far easier, and far more affordable, than fixing one later.
