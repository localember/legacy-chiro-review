TITLE: Layered Powerhouse Salad Recipe | Legacy Chiropractic Cary NC
H1: Layered Powerhouse Salad, a healthy recipe from Legacy Chiropractic in Cary
A filling, plant-forward bowl of quinoa, lentils, roasted butternut squash and greens. Serves 4.

## Ingredients
- 1 cup raw quinoa (rinse first; it cooks in about 20 minutes)
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 1 cup cooked French lentils (buy them precooked or cook your own in about 20 minutes)
- 1 butternut squash, cubed
- 1 tablespoon coconut oil (extra virgin, cold-pressed)
- 2 heaping tablespoons tahini (thin with lemon juice if needed)
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
- Pinch of black pepper
- Pinch of Himalayan or sea salt
- 2 large handfuls baby kale, spinach or your favorite greens

## Topping
- 2 tablespoons raw pumpkin seeds
- A drizzle of tahini
- A splash of lemon juice
- 1/2 avocado (optional)

## Steps
1. <b>Roast the squash.</b> Heat the oven to 375°F. Peel and cube the squash, or buy it already cubed. Toss with coconut oil and a pinch each of salt and pepper, spread on parchment or foil on an oven-safe tray, and bake 20 to 25 minutes. Turn the pieces and keep roasting until a fork slides in easily.
2. <b>Cook the grains and greens.</b> While the squash roasts, cook the quinoa and lentils. They keep well, so making a batch ahead for several meals is handy. Steam or sauté the greens.
3. <b>Mix.</b> Combine the cooked quinoa and lentils, then season them with turmeric, black pepper, tahini and lemon juice.
4. <b>Layer.</b> In a wide bowl, start with squash, then the quinoa and lentil mix, then greens, and repeat. Finish with pumpkin seeds and more tahini and lemon. Add avocado on top right before serving; if packing it to go, add the avocado last.
