## Changes start right away
Your body begins changing as soon as you become pregnant, all to support the new life growing inside you. As beautiful as pregnancy is, it can bring discomfort and pain. Morning sickness is very common in the first trimester, driven by rising hormone levels and the growing placenta.

That surge of hormones and placental growth makes the liver work much harder, which puts it under real stress. At Legacy Chiropractic we work to remove stress on the nervous system and help the liver function at its best as it handles the extra workload.

## Headaches
Headaches are common in the first trimester and can continue throughout pregnancy. Possible causes include misalignments in the neck and upper back, dehydration, stress, and withdrawal from sugar and caffeine. Adjustments, good nutrition and healthy sleep can greatly reduce or end these headaches.

## The second trimester
As your baby bump appears, your body is adapting to carry the extra weight. If you already have spinal misalignments, pregnancy can make the symptoms worse, including headaches, acid reflux, constipation, diarrhea or pain. Throughout pregnancy the pelvis tips forward and changes the curve of your low back. That is normal, but an existing misalignment in your low back or spine can turn it into significant pain and complications.

## Labor and delivery
The biggest benefits of prenatal chiropractic show up in labor. Average labor lasts 10 and a half hours. Most important, women who have had regular care often find they do not need drugs during delivery, because they have better prepared their bodies for it.
