## Where the word comes from
"Chiropractic" comes from two Greek words, cheir (hand) and praktos (done), meaning "done by hand." Daniel David Palmer, the founder of chiropractic, chose them. In 1895 he adjusted a janitor named Harvey Lillard, who was mostly deaf at the time, and Lillard later reported that his hearing improved after the adjustment. Palmer opened the first chiropractic school two years later. Over the century since, chiropractors have used adjustments for headaches and migraines, strained muscles, sore backs, carpal tunnel and much more, and his work still touches millions of lives.
