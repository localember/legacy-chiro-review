## Vitamin C
You have likely heard that you should take vitamin C when you are sick. It is a vital nutrient your body cannot make, so it must come from your diet. Surprisingly, most of us eat more than enough vitamin C but cannot use it well. Vitamin C and glucose (sugar) enter your cells through the same doorway, because their structures are nearly identical in shape and form. The catch is that your body always lets sugar in first. With too much sugar in your system, vitamin C waits its turn, which leaves your cells deficient. Cells short on vitamin C mount a weaker defense, which leaves the door open for germs such as the flu. On top of that, sugar feeds infection.

The best move is to cut back on sugar, which means candy, soda and processed grains. Raising your vitamin C intake saturates your body so it pushes its way in ahead of sugar, which can help when you are sick.

## Vitamin D3
This nutrient is key for strong bones and for a strong immune system. Unlike vitamin C, your body can make D3 with help from sunlight. In winter, though, sunlight is scarcer, making it hard for people to make enough. Meat, dairy and eggs also supply D3. Some people benefit from taking 1000IU of vitamin D3 daily.

## Rest
Most of your body's growth and repair happens while you sleep. Sleep is not a vitamin, but it is just as essential. Everyone needs a different amount; if you wake up feeling rested, you are getting enough quality sleep. Try different bedtimes until you find what works. For most adults, 8 hours is the sweet spot.

## Exercise
Movement is life. Regular activity keeps you in condition and gets your blood moving. Poor circulation makes it hard for your body to deliver germ fighting cells to infected areas and hard to clear the waste left behind by an infection.

## De-stress
When you are emotionally stressed, your immune defenses drop. You may not be able to remove every source of stress, but you can find healthy ways to handle it. Talk things through with a family member, friend or counselor.

## Probiotics
Probiotic literally means "for life." These friendly gut bacteria are among the first defenders your body has. Take a probiotic supplement or eat cultured foods like sauerkraut and yogurt. Cut the sugar, though, since it feeds harmful bacteria.

## Omega-3 fats
You hear about omega-3s everywhere now, and for good reason. They are essential building blocks of your nervous system and they fight inflammation. Low omega-3 levels limit your body's ability to mount a healthy immune response when it needs to. Good sources are flax seed, dark leafy greens, grass fed meat, eggs and cold water fish.
