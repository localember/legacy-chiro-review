## What TMJ is
TMJ disorder is a group of conditions affecting the temporomandibular joint, which connects your jaw to your skull. When the muscles or ligaments that move this joint have problems, the joint can act up.

Common signs include pain in the jaw, cheek, teeth or in front of the ear when chewing or yawning; clicking in the jaw; being unable to open wide, or the jaw locking once it is open; and upper and lower teeth that no longer line up when you close your mouth. Some people also get headaches, hearing problems, dizziness or earaches.

## What causes it
TMJ has many possible causes, and we will look into each of them at your visit. Known causes include serious jaw injury or dislocation, physical or emotional stress, and even long dental appointments with your mouth held wide open. Emotional stress does not cause TMJ by itself, but if it makes you clench your teeth, that clenching can. Women are four times as likely as men to have TMJ.
