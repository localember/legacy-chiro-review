H1: Healing takes time: a note from our Cary chiropractic office
Many of you first came to Legacy Chiropractic in a crisis. You wanted to be fixed and you wanted your symptoms gone. That is understandable, and it is also why this lesson matters in every part of life.

## Look for the root cause
Now that you have learned more about how your body works, we hope you see why getting to the root of a health crisis matters, and why covering up a symptom is a short-term answer to a long-term problem that may have been building for years. One adjustment cannot undo years of trouble.

## Healing is a process
Give that process the respect it deserves. Rome was not built in a day, and neither was your body, your family, your home, your relationships or your skill at your work. The list goes on.

So why expect anything different when your body stops adapting and a symptom or health crisis appears? Think about conditions like high blood pressure, heart disease, high cholesterol, digestive trouble, obesity, low back pain, migraines, vertigo, fertility issues, asthma or numbness. Each one took a long time to develop. It makes sense that they do not heal overnight either.

## How chiropractic fits in
Your nerve system controls how your body works, including every condition above. We need to make sure messages travel clearly from your brain to your body and back again so your body can heal itself. That is how chiropractic works.
