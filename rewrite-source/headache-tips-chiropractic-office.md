H1: Headache tips from our Cary chiropractic office
Nearly everyone in Cary gets a headache now and then. Most need no medical care and fade on their own. When a headache starts getting in the way of your day, or takes it over entirely, it is time to get professional help. These tips can help you find relief and stay ahead of the problem.

## Why does my head hurt?
Many different triggers can cause headaches. Some relate to lifestyle, some to an underlying health condition, some to genetics and some to posture. Common ones include certain foods or smells, bright lights, loud noise, excessive exercise, blood sugar swings, the wrong pillow, poor posture at work and poor sleep habits.

In about 95% of cases, a headache is not caused by an underlying disease or health problem. Most come from muscle tension in the neck or upper back after long hours held in one position. That means almost every headache can be treated by changing habits and seeing a Cary chiropractor.

## Help yourself first
The biggest tip we can give is to take action yourself. If your job keeps you sitting still for hours, make yourself get up and move about every 30 minutes. It does not need to be much; a lap around the office or some light stretching works, as long as you are not sitting.

If you tend to get dull, throbbing headaches, skip heavy exercise until they are under control. Keep working out, but lean toward low impact activities. Avoid clenching your teeth while exercising or at any time, since it strains the jaw joints. Dehydration is a major trigger, so drink water all day.
