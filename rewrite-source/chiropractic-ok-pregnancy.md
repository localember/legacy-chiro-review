H1: Is chiropractic safe during pregnancy? A Cary chiropractor answers
People argue about whether chiropractic care during pregnancy is safe and worthwhile. Some say it puts mother and baby at risk, while others say it is safe and brings real benefits. In fact, mothers under chiropractic care generally have smoother pregnancies and births. Here is what you should know.

## Chiropractic and pregnancy
Chiropractic is safe and helpful for babies, toddlers, teens and adults, and it is just as safe and helpful during pregnancy. It poses no danger to the baby, and the benefits for the expecting mom are significant. Cary chiropractors who complete the required training and education for licensure are trained to care for pregnant women. Some focus on prenatal adjusting and may have more experience, but most have the skills and knowledge needed.

Ask any mom: carrying a baby is hard on your back, neck, hips and knees. Even daily tasks get harder as the shifting weight pulls parts of the body out of balance. Regular adjustments during pregnancy relieve much of the extra stress on your spine and can keep nerves from being pinched, which can cause pain, numbness or tingling in your back, hips, legs, arms or fingers. Your posture shifts week by week as the baby grows, and regular adjustments help correct the imbalances that follow.

## What you can do
- <b>Stay active.</b> Walking and swimming keep blood moving and muscles working throughout pregnancy. Check with your doctor first if you were not active before you became pregnant.
- <b>Skip the heels.</b> They make you less stable and can worsen postural imbalances. Choose flat, sensible shoes whenever you can.
- <b>Lift carefully.</b> When lifting children or anything else, always bend your knees, and ask for help if it feels too heavy.
- <b>Sleep on your side</b> with a pillow between your knees to take pressure off your low back.
- <b>At a desk,</b> use a footrest to ease your feet and legs, and get up and move at least every 30 minutes.
- <b>After delivery,</b> schedule adjustments to support healing while your muscles, tendons and ligaments return to normal.
