For huge numbers of people, headaches are part of everyday life. Some come once in a while, others often. Some are a dull throb and others are sharp and intense. They have many causes, and almost all of them get in the way of life, from a little trouble focusing to lying perfectly still in a dark room. In most cases, a Cary chiropractor can ease your symptoms and help you get back to normal.

## Common triggers
There are several types of headaches, so there are many triggers, including bright light, loud noise, certain foods, stress, blood sugar swings, high blood pressure, chemical exposure, caffeine, neck tension and spinal misalignment. Neck tension headaches are among the most common, brought on by poor posture, repetitive motions and other physical activity.

## Prevention tips
Your chiropractor can relieve most headaches, and these proven habits let you help yourself too:
- Change positions about every 30 minutes, whatever you are doing. If you have to stay put, gently stretch your neck every 30 minutes to head off tension headaches.
- Stop clenching your teeth. Many people do it without noticing, and it can trigger headaches.
- If your workouts seem to bring on headaches, try something lower impact. There are plenty of good ways to exercise, so pick one that does not give you a headache.
- Sip water throughout the day, since dehydration is a known trigger.
