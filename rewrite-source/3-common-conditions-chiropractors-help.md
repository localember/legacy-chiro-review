H1: Three common problems our Cary chiropractors help with
Over the last few decades chiropractic has gone from a fringe option to a mainstream one. Skeptics will always exist, but millions of happy patients worldwide make a strong case for how well it works. Adjustments can help with dozens of health problems. Here are three we see all the time in Cary.

## 1. Headaches
Everyone gets a headache now and then. Some are a dull nuisance and some are severe, but any of them can wreck your day. Whether it is a tension headache from a long day at the desk or a migraine tied to a health condition, it is hard to think about anything else once it starts.

Our Cary chiropractors help people with headache pain every week. Often the pain in your head actually comes from vertebrae in your spine that are out of alignment. After gentle adjustments and regular upkeep, many patients tell us the headaches stop, and so does their need for pain pills.

## 2. Shoulder pain
Far too many people live with sore shoulders. The pain may be referred from misalignments in the spine, or it may come from a misalignment in the shoulder itself.

Whatever kind of shoulder pain you have, our team can help ease both the pain and any loss of motion. We will assess your shoulder and give you our best advice on what you can do to fix the problem.

## 3. Lower back pain
If headaches and sore shoulders are common, low back pain may top them all. Misaligned spinal segments are one of the most frequent causes of pain in the lumbar spine.

The Legacy Chiropractic team can help with the pain and stiffness that come with lumbar sprains and strains, so you can get back to your normal life. Even if you have hurt for years and been told nothing will help, it is worth exploring your options.
