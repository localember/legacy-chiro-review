H1: Back pain tips from a Cary chiropractor
The back may be the body part that gives people the most trouble over a lifetime. Back pain is so common that many people treat it as normal and just push through. Anyone who knows chiropractic, or human anatomy, knows most back pain does not have to happen. Chiropractic care can help a great deal, and there is plenty you can do yourself too.

## Lift with care
Bad lifting form is a leading cause of low back sprains and strains, so technique matters with anything heavy. Keep your back straight and let your legs do most of the work. When we get tired we tend to use our legs less and less, and that is exactly when backs get hurt. Do not twist while holding something heavy. If you need to turn, move your feet until you face the right way.

## Eat well
Few people think of nutrition as part of spinal health, but it can be. Plenty of water and healthy food help keep weight off your middle. Extra weight in the belly throws off your balance and tugs on your low back muscles all day. If you have back pain and carry weight around your stomach, changing your diet can lighten the load on your back.

## Exercise sensibly
Exercise matters for many reasons, but done carelessly it is also an easy way to hurt your back. Whatever you choose, warm up first and cool down afterward. Many people jump straight into lifting or a game while their back muscles are still cold and tight. Pick activities that match your fitness level and look after your back before, during and after.

## Do not sit too long
Most of us sit far more than people used to, and long hours at a computer take a toll. Your body is built to move, and sitting all day squeezes the discs in your spine.
