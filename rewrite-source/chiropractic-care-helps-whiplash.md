Whiplash is one of the injuries we see most often after car accidents in Cary. When someone books a chiropractic visit after a crash, it is frequently because of whiplash pain. Here is how our office can help.

## What whiplash is
Whiplash is the medical term for a neck injury caused by a car accident. Rear-end collisions are among the most common crashes and the most likely to cause it. When you are struck from behind, your head snaps backward and then forward, and that motion causes the injury.

## How we diagnose it
We evaluate your whole spine to confirm the neck is the only area hurt. We look for limited range of motion, muscle spasm, weakness and pain. In the early, acute stage you may not be able to turn your head comfortably, and you may have a headache. We also feel along your neck and back for tender, tight spots.

## How we treat it
While whiplash is still acute, we may use gentle stretching, ultrasound and other methods to bring down inflammation, and we may suggest ice to reduce swelling. Once the swelling settles and the pain eases, care shifts to spinal adjustments that restore normal motion and blood flow to the neck.
