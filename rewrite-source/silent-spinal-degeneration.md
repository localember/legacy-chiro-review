H1: Silent spinal degeneration: why a Cary spinal checkup matters before pain starts
<b>Myth:</b> If I had a back problem, I would feel pain.

<b>Fact:</b> You can have a spinal problem like disc degeneration with no pain whatsoever.

## What the x-ray showed
A 50 year old man came to us with shoulder pain only. He reported no back pain at all. His x-ray, though, showed major disc degeneration below the 5th lumbar vertebra: the space between the 5th vertebra and the tailbone was very thin. Degeneration like that can take more than 20 years to develop. By comparison, the space between the 4th and 5th vertebrae looked much closer to a normal, healthy disc.

## Why it matters
The degeneration may cause no symptoms today, but left uncorrected it will likely cause complications later. The nerve root leaving the spine between the 5th vertebra and the sacrum is the communication line to the buttocks, reproductive organs, bladder, prostate, lower legs and feet.
