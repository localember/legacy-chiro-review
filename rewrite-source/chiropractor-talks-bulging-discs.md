## What is a bulging disc?
A bulging disc diagnosis means part of a disc is pushing out past the outer edge of the vertebra. That makes it more likely the disc will press on a nerve or other tissue. Bulging discs are common and often cause no symptoms, but in some people they can cause disabling pain.

They can come from sports injuries or other trauma, like a hard fall or a car accident. Normal aging can cause them too, because the outer layer of the disc weakens over time. When pressure from the disc's center pushes out against that outer edge, a bulge can form.

## Symptoms
Symptoms start when the disc presses on spinal nerves. Close to 90% of bulging discs are in the lumbar spine, the low back, and pain may spread from there into the hips, buttocks and legs and down to the feet. A bulging disc in the neck can send pain from the neck down the arms and into the fingers. At its worst, a bulging disc can cause pain so severe you can barely move.
