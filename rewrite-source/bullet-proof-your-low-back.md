H1: How to bulletproof your low back, from a Cary chiropractor
A rock-solid low back starts with a healthy spine and nervous system. Three things keep them that way:
- Regular adjustments on a steady rhythm
- Dropping bad habits
- A strong core

Here are five at-home exercises to build that core.

## Lunges
- Stand tall and step forward with one foot.
- Lower until your front knee is bent to about 90 degrees.
- Keep your back upright.
- Drop your back knee toward the floor without letting it touch.
- Push back up and bring your feet together.

## Squats
- Stand with feet about shoulder width apart and your back straight.
- Bend your knees and sit back as if lowering into a chair.
- Keep your knees lined up over your ankles.

## Planks
- Lie face down, propped on your elbows.
- Keep palms flat and balance on your toes.
- Hold your body in one straight line from head to heels.
- Tighten your stomach muscles.

## Push-ups
- Place your hands a little wider than your shoulders.
- Balance on your toes, or on your knees to make it easier.
- Keep a straight line from shoulders through knees to ankles.
- Squeeze your glutes and abs.
- Bend your elbows until your chest is just off the floor.
- Press back up by straightening your arms.

## Jumping jacks
- Start with feet together and arms by your sides.
- Bend your knees slightly and jump, spreading your feet and raising your arms overhead.
- Jump again to bring your arms down and your feet back together.
