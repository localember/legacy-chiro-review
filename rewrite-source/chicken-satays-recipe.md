TITLE: Chicken Satay Recipe from a Cary Chiropractor
H1: A Cary chiropractor's favorite chicken satay recipe
These chicken satays have been a Ballam family favorite for years. The recipe comes from the cookbook Food to Live By, and it is a fun change of pace for chicken night. And honestly, peanut butter makes everything better.

## Why these spices are good for you
- <b>Turmeric</b> gives curry its yellow color and is rated among the healthiest foods in the world. It fights inflammation powerfully and has shown cancer-fighting potential. Research also links it to better liver function, lower cholesterol and protection against Alzheimer's disease.
- <b>Coriander</b> has long been called an "anti-diabetic" spice for its ability to help control blood sugar. It also helps lower cholesterol and calm inflammation.
- <b>Cumin</b> is rich in vitamins, minerals and antioxidants, supports the immune system and helps digestion.

## Ingredients for the satays
- Curry powder, 2 tablespoons
- Ground coriander, 1 tablespoon
- Cumin, 1 teaspoon
- Sugar, 1 tablespoon
- Salt, half a teaspoon
- Chicken, 1 pound of boneless, skinless breasts or thighs, sliced into strips about three quarters of an inch wide
- Canned coconut milk, 1 and a half cups
- Rice vinegar, 1 and a half tablespoons
- Peanut or coconut oil, three quarters of a cup

## Ingredients for the peanut sauce
- Coconut milk, 1 cup
- Unsweetened peanut butter, 3 tablespoons
- Curry powder, 1 tablespoon

## How to make them
Stir together the curry powder, coriander, cumin, sugar and salt and set the bowl aside. Slide two or three chicken strips onto each skewer, then roll each skewer in the spice blend until it is evenly coated. Let them sit at room temperature for 15 minutes to soak up the spices.

While you wait, whisk the coconut milk, vinegar and oil in a small bowl, then pour it over the skewers to marinate. The cookbook calls for at least 2 hours of marinating. We rarely plan that far ahead, so ours usually get a short soak, and they still come out great.

Heat the grill to medium-high and cook the satays, flipping once, until the chicken is done all the way through. Serve with the peanut sauce.
