H1: Improve your golf game with chiropractic care in Cary
Seeing a Cary chiropractor will not cure your slice or sink your putts, but it may help your overall game and bring your score down. Golf is famously tough on the low back. The fast, repeated twist of every swing puts your back at risk each round, and if your back is already injured, other muscle groups are at risk too.

## How chiropractic helps your game
Chiropractors are trained to treat the whole neuromusculoskeletal system, so they can help golfers cut down the stress and strain on their bodies. The low back takes a lot of torque in a normal swing, but other areas matter too. Pain or limited motion in your shoulders, elbows, knees or wrists will throw off your swing and push your scores up.

Adjustments realign the body, take pressure off compressed nerves and get more blood to the muscles you rely on. When you are pain free and moving well, you can swing freely and focus on the game.

## It starts with you
Chiropractic can help, and your own habits can help even more:
- Get to the course early to stretch and take some easy swings before you play. Practice range or not, loosening up is key. Stretch lightly after your round too, to keep muscles loose and long.
- Stay hydrated. It is easy to get dehydrated on the course, and that sets you up for a strained muscle or similar injury. Drink plenty of water before, during and after your round, especially in the heat.
- If you walk, pull your bag instead of carrying it. Lugging a heavy bag around all 18 holes is a recipe for disc trouble and irritated nerves.
- Skip metal spikes when you can. They can catch in the ground mid swing and cause a serious knee or back injury.
- If your swing causes pain anywhere, think about lessons to learn a more efficient swing.
