Our core method

Gonstead chiropractic in Cary

Gonstead is a way of working rather than a single move. The doctor gathers several kinds of evidence, and only adjusts where they all point to the same vertebra.

Why precision matters more than force

A general manipulation can move a lot of joints at once. A Gonstead adjustment aims at one segment, in one direction, with only as much pressure as that joint needs. Patients tend to describe it as calm and deliberate. There is very little twisting, which is part of why we can see people of nearly every age.

The five checks before an adjustment

  • Posture scan. A digital posture analysis shows how your body is compensating.
  • Temperature reading. A dual-probe instrument compares the two sides of the spine, which can flag irritated areas.
  • Hands-on at rest. Static palpation picks up swelling, tenderness and changes in muscle tone.
  • Hands-on in motion. Motion palpation finds joints that are stuck.
  • Full-spine X-rays. Standing films from the front and side show the whole spine at once.

Built around you, not a script

Your history and symptoms are part of the picture too. The doctors put all of it together, walk you through the findings in plain language, and then suggest a care plan. You always know what we found and why we are recommending it.
