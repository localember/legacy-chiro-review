Website privacy policy for Legacy Chiropractic in Cary

Here is how Legacy Chiropractic handles and safeguards the details you share through this website. Your privacy is important to us, and anything we ask for that could identify you will be handled only as this policy describes. From time to time we may revise the policy by editing this page, so it is worth looking back occasionally to be sure the changes work for you. This policy has applied since January 14, 2013.

What we may ask for

  • Your name and your job title
  • Ways to reach you, such as an email address
  • Background details like your postcode, preferences and interests
  • Anything else connected to surveys or offers we run

How we use it

These details help us understand what you are looking for and do a better job for you. Specifically, we may use them to:

  • Maintain our own records.
  • Make our products and services better.
  • Email you now and then, at the address you gave us, about new offerings, specials or other news we expect you will like.
  • Reach out by email, phone, fax or mail to ask for your opinion as part of market research.
  • Shape the website around what interests you.
  • Pass your details to third-party partners of ours for their marketing or promotions.

Selling your information is something we will never do.

Security

We take care to keep what you share safe. Physical, electronic and administrative safeguards are in place to stop anyone from reaching or revealing online information without permission.

About cookies

Cookies are tiny files a website asks to save on your device. If you allow it, the file is stored and can help measure site traffic or recognize a return visit. Cookies let a site treat you as an individual, for example by remembering your preferences, likes and dislikes.

We rely on traffic log cookies to learn which of our pages get visited. The numbers help us study traffic patterns and make the site more useful. We look at this data only in summary form for statistics, then delete it.

Put simply, cookies show us which pages help visitors and which do not, so we can keep improving. They do not let us into your device, and they reveal nothing about you other than what you decide to give us.

Accepting cookies is your choice. Browsers usually allow them by default, though you can typically switch that off in your settings. If you do, some parts of the site may not work as well.

Leaving our site

We sometimes link to outside websites that may be helpful. After you follow one of those links, the other site is outside our control, so we cannot be held responsible for how it treats anything you share there, and this policy no longer applies. Take care, and check that site's own privacy terms.

Your choices

You can limit how we collect and use your personal details:

  • On any form on this website, look for the checkbox that lets you opt out of having your details used for direct marketing.
  • If you said yes to direct marketing in the past and have changed your mind, write to us or email drballam@legacychiropracticnc.com whenever you like.

Without your consent, or a legal requirement, we will not sell, hand out or rent your personal details to anyone else. If you ask us to, we may send you promotions from other companies we think you would like.

The Data Protection Act 1998 lets you ask what personal details we keep about you; a small fee applies. To get a copy, write to drballam@legacychiropracticnc.com.

Spot something wrong or missing in the information we have about you? Let us know in writing or by email at that same address, and we will fix any errors quickly.
