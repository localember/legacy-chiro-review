Chiropractic care for back pain in Cary

Back pain, low back pain in particular, is a daily reality for many people in Cary and everywhere else. Chiropractors have long been thought of as "back pain doctors." We help with far more than backs, but the nickname fits. Our Cary office offers a range of personalized back pain treatments aimed at getting rid of the pain for good.

Back pain by the numbers

  • By one estimate, about 31 million Americans have back pain.
  • The 2010 Global Burden of Disease report ranked low back pain as the top cause of disability worldwide.
  • Back pain is among the most common reasons people miss work, and it is the second most common reason for doctor visits, after upper respiratory infections.
  • Half of all working Americans report back pain symptoms every year.
  • Most back pain is mechanical, meaning it does not come from an underlying disease.
  • A conservative estimate puts American spending on back pain at about 0 billion.
  • Some experts say up to 80% of people will have back pain at some point.

Why does my back hurt?

Causes vary widely. Your back is a complex structure of muscles, bones, ligaments and joints working together, so strains and other injuries are common. Lifting the wrong way, poor posture, stress, extra weight, an old sports injury or a car accident can all be behind it. Arthritis and osteoporosis cause back pain too, as can disease in certain internal organs.

How chiropractic helps

Chiropractic is a safe, effective treatment for many kinds of back pain. With the right care, you can expect to move more freely and have fewer flare-ups. Many patients also appreciate getting away from the side effects of prescription pain medicine, a benefit they had not even thought about. Chiropractic targets the cause of back pain, while most conventional care targets symptoms. Treating only the pain may bring relief, but the source of that pain is still there.

A good chiropractor will also recommend lifestyle changes to keep back pain from coming back: keep a healthy weight, avoid sitting for long periods, stay active for life, drink enough water, wear low heels, stretch, and use good posture. With your own effort and quality chiropractic care, back pain can become a thing of the past. See our back pain page for more.
