How to choose the right pillow, from a Cary chiropractor

Patients often ask me which pillow I recommend. I do not work for any pillow company, but I do have advice on picking the best one for you. In short, the right pillow is whichever one leaves your spine free of subluxations. Here is what that means.

Why sleep matters to your spine

My job as a chiropractor is to find and correct subluxations in your spine, and if I spot ways you may be adding new ones, I will tell you. Subluxations come from trauma, bad habits, or both, and for many people sleep position is one of those habits.

Plenty of people love sleeping on their stomach, but it is one of the hardest positions on your spine. You have to turn your face to the side to breathe, which keeps steady stress on the neck and mid back and causes subluxations for many people.

Best positions

I recommend a position that keeps your spine as neutral as possible. Sleeping on your back or side are two of the best choices. Side sleepers should keep shoulders and hips stacked in line; it is tempting to roll the hips forward, which twists the low back.

Picking the pillow

Choose a pillow that supports your neck. Many people use one that is too thick, tilting the head up, or too thin, letting the head sag.
