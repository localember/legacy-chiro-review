The YWTL postural exercise, from a Cary chiropractor

Most of us know postural exercises are good for us and that we should do more of them. Finding time is the hard part. Here is a single exercise that fits anywhere in your schedule.

Why your core matters

A strong core is essential for supporting your spine. We are built to move every day: walking, bending, twisting, lifting and breaking a sweat. Most Americans spend more time sitting than doing any of that. We cannot skip those movements, because they build the core strength that supports the spine and helps keep it aligned. Weak core muscles leave the spine vulnerable, so even a small bump can cause a subluxation, a spinal misalignment that disrupts communication in your nervous system. Postural exercises cannot correct a subluxation, but they do help prevent them.

Build the core, protect the spine, and lower your odds of a subluxation.

The YWTL exercise

This simple move is something anyone can do several times a day to counter the strain of long hours in a chair. Its name describes the arm shapes you make: Y, W, T and L. Ask us to show you at your next visit.
