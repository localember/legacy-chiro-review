Cary chiropractors on headaches, migraines and the spine

Symptoms are signals. They are how the body tells us a deeper problem exists. A headache, like any symptom, is basically an alarm telling us to act. Migraines are severe, recurring headaches that sometimes come with other symptoms, like nausea or visual changes such as an aura.

Masking is not fixing

Covering up a symptom can bring short-term relief, but it does not fix the real problem. It actually lets us ignore that problem while it worsens. Medication can give temporary relief, yet it often just masks symptoms and may cause serious side effects.

Side effects from headache prescriptions can include nausea, vomiting, dizziness, drowsiness, diarrhea, heartburn, constipation, liver damage, kidney damage, stomach pain, shortness of breath, weight gain, high blood pressure, blood sugar problems, insomnia, fatigue, memory problems, anxiety, sun sensitivity and leg pain, among others. And one more: headaches.

The numbers

Headache figures in the U.S. are staggering. According to the American Headache Society:

  • Someone in the United States visits the emergency room with a headache or migraine every 10 seconds.
  • Americans spend more than $1 billion a year on over-the-counter headache and migraine medicine.
  • Work time lost to migraines costs the U.S. more than $13 billion each year.

Finding the root cause

Chiropractic can help people with migraines reach the root of the problem instead of only treating symptoms. At Legacy Chiropractic we use the Gonstead method, a very specific and gentle approach that evaluates the entire spine through detailed analysis, including X-rays, nervous system analysis and palpation.

In our analysis we often find migraines tied to two key spinal areas: the base of the neck and the middle of the low back. These areas relate neurologically to hormone production. When they are irritated, hormones can fall out of balance and trigger migraines, which is why migraines are sometimes called hormonal headaches. Once these areas are addressed, most people's symptoms get better.

Hope and a choice

I use specific adjustments to correct these key areas, an approach that has proven effective with seniors, children and pregnant women as well as everyone else. I truly believe we are designed for life, health and vitality, and we should expect nothing less. Again and again I meet people in pain who have been told to manage their symptoms with nothing done about the cause. I want my patients to have hope and to know they can choose a different path for their health today. I look forward to showing you what chiropractic has to offer.
