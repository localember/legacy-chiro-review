A Cary chiropractor on spinal degeneration and time

Albert Einstein once said, "The only reason for time is so that everything does not happen at once."

Older than you think

Most people start chiropractic care because of a specific symptom: low back pain, headaches, disc problems, sciatica, stiffness, back spasms and more. Many assume the problem began recently, when the symptoms did. They are often surprised when we go over their X-rays and the problem turns out to be decades old. X-rays let you actually see time at work. When we classify spinal degeneration on them, they show how old the problem is, revealing bone spurs, disc degeneration and compensating curves. Those changes did not happen overnight; they built up over time, often silently.

Not just normal aging

Spinal degeneration is graded from Phase 1 to Phase 3 and can start developing in as little as 5 years. Many people think degeneration is just part of getting older. In fact it has little to do with normal aging and a lot to do with how the spine functions. Subluxations make the spine break down faster. Put simply, a subluxation is a misalignment of spinal bones that reduces communication in your nervous system (there is much more to say about subluxations, and that deserves its own post). It is common to see X-rays where only a couple of areas show Phase 2 degeneration and the rest of the spine is normal.

Every bone in your spine is the same age, so something must have happened to the areas that wore out faster.

Get your spine checked

Most people get regular dental checkups to catch cavities, but most have never had a chiropractor check their spine. We recommend that everyone have their spine evaluated by a chiropractor. You are never too young or too old to have a subluxation, and unless your spine has been checked, there is no way to know from how you feel. Questions? Call our Cary office at (919) 363-2277.
