Measure twice, adjust once

Plenty of offices crack every joint that feels tight. We do the opposite. Our doctors practice the Gonstead method exclusively, which means gathering several kinds of evidence and then adjusting only the segment where it all lines up. The result is a visit that feels unhurried and an adjustment that is light and specific.

You will spend real time with your doctor, learn what is going on in plain language, and leave with a plan made for you, whether you are seven or seventy.

Your first visits, step by step

  1. TalkA free 15-minute conversation about your history and goals.
  2. ExaminePosture scan, temperature reading, hands-on checks and full-spine X-rays when needed.
  3. ExplainWe show you what we found and lay out a written plan.
  4. AdjustSpecific, low-force adjustments where the findings agree.
