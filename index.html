Welcome to Legacy Chiropractic in Cary

Plenty of offices crack every joint that feels tight. We take the opposite approach. Our doctors use the Gonstead method exclusively, so we collect several kinds of evidence first and then adjust only the segment where it all lines up. Visits feel unhurried, and adjustments are light and specific.

What can a Cary chiropractor help with?

People come to us for back and neck pain, headaches, sciatica, disc problems, injuries from car accidents or work, pregnancy aches and simple tune-ups to stay active. Whatever brings you in, you will learn what is going on in plain language and leave with a plan made for you.

Our approach

Why choose a Gonstead chiropractor in Cary?

Gonstead care is built on measuring before acting. Instead of a routine that treats every back the same, your doctor looks for the one or two places that are actually stuck and leaves the rest alone.

That precision is what lets us keep adjustments gentle, with very little twisting, for children, expecting moms, athletes and older adults alike.

Care plans are written for you, with individual, family and corporate options, plus simple coaching on sleep, sitting and lifting between visits.

Common questions about chiropractic care in Cary

What happens at my first visit?

After a short conversation about your history and goals, the doctor works through the Gonstead exam: posture, a temperature reading along the spine, hands-on checks and X-rays when they are needed. You get a clear explanation of what we found before any adjustment.

Is a Gonstead adjustment gentle?

It is specific rather than forceful. We aim at one segment in one direction, with very little twisting, which is why we care for patients from infants to grandparents.

Will I need X-rays?

Only if the doctor thinks they will change your care. When we take them, they are standing, full-spine views taken right here in the office.

Do you see kids and expecting moms?

Yes. Dr. Ballam trained in pediatric technique through the ICPA, and we position expecting moms so there is no pressure on the belly. Newborns of our prenatal families get a complimentary first screening.

Do you take insurance?

We are in network with Cigna, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, MedCost, HealthSpring, HealthTeam Advantage and Liberty Advantage, and we will check your benefits for free.

What is the free consultation?

Fifteen minutes one-on-one with the doctor to talk about your concerns and goals. It does not include an exam, X-rays or an adjustment, and there is no obligation.
