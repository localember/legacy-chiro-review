Free new patient consultation in Cary

Not sure chiropractic is right for you? Sit down with one of our doctors for 15 minutes, free, and find out.

What you get

  • A 15-minute, one-on-one conversation with the doctor
  • Time to go over your history, your concerns and your goals
  • An honest answer about whether we can help

What it does not include

The consultation does not include an exam, X-rays or an adjustment, and there is no obligation to start care. It is simply a chance to meet us before you commit to anything.
